This is SKUNK

A Standalone Phishing Email Analyzer for your desktop.  This app will connect to your email and provide you with a risk rating for each email you recieve.

The goal for this project is to help users prevent and ignore phishing emails and stop potential cyberattacks.

Skunk operates by scanning each part of your email while searching for suspicious activity included in the email.  Some examples are return addresses, sender addresses, links, files, and the overall tone of the email are all taken into account when Skunk reviews emails.
